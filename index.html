About the event

Two conferences, one week in Rabat

After Sydney 2022, Kashiwa 2025 and Sofia 2026, the joint 3D GeoInfo and Smart Data Smart Cities conference comes to Africa for the first time. Hosted by IAV Hassan II in Rabat, the capital of Morocco, the event brings together researchers, practitioners, industry and government to advance 3D geoinformation and data-driven smart cities.

22nd International 3D GeoInfo Conference

The 3D GeoInfo conference aims to bring together international researchers, practitioners and professionals from academia, industry and government to discuss the latest advances in 3D geoinformation science and technology. This annual event offers a multidisciplinary and inter-sectoral forum in the fields of 3D/4D data collection, management, data quality, analysis, advanced modelling and visualisation, with a strong focus on cutting-edge research, standardisation, implementation and application issues across different disciplines.

Organised under the umbrella of ISPRS. Proceedings in ISPRS Annals and Archives.

11th International Smart Data and Smart Cities Conference

Smart Data and Smart Cities (SDSC) is an annual international conference that brings together experts in city analytics, GIS, digital twins, smart cities and data science from leading research institutes to discuss advances in methods and applications. It provides a forum to discuss the use of data and technology to plan and shape our future cities, from sensing and open data platforms to citizen participation and net-zero urban development.

Initiated by the Urban Data Management Society (UDMS). Proceedings in ISPRS Annals and Archives.

Instructions for authors

Full papers → ISPRS Annals

Original research papers of 6 to 8 pages in the ISPRS template. Papers undergo double-blind peer review by at least two reviewers and must be fully anonymised at submission. Accepted papers are published in the ISPRS Annals of the Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences.

Extended abstracts → ISPRS Archives

Extended abstracts of 2 pages (800–1000 words), double-blind reviewed. Authors of accepted abstracts submit a full paper of 6 to 8 pages for publication in the ISPRS Archives.

Applied & industry session

Extended abstracts presenting practical applications, city projects and industry solutions. Emphasis is on real-world deployment rather than research novelty. Presentation only, or optional publication in the Archives.

How to submit
  1. Prepare your manuscript with the official ISPRS guidelines and templates (Word or LaTeX).
  2. Remove all author-identifying information for the review version.
  3. Submit through ConfTool, selecting the track (3D GeoInfo or SDSC) and the submission type.
  4. After acceptance, upload the camera-ready, non-anonymised version and complete the Copernicus publishing agreement.
  5. At least one author must register by the author registration deadline for the paper to be included in the proceedings and programme.

ISPRS Annals and Archives are indexed in Scopus, Web of Science (Conference Proceedings Citation Index), Ei Compendex and DOAJ. Selected papers will be invited to extended versions in partner journals (see Partners).

Best paper awards

Best paper and best presentation awards will be given in both the 3D GeoInfo and SDSC tracks, with a dedicated student award.

    Venue

    Institut Agronomique et Vétérinaire Hassan II

    IAV Hassan II is Morocco's leading institution for agronomy, veterinary sciences and geomatics, and home to the Department of Geodesy and Surveying (Filière de Formation en Topographie). The campus sits in Madinat Al Irfane, the university district of Rabat, next to Mohammed V University and the National School of Computer Science (ENSIAS), a few minutes from the Agdal and Hay Riad business districts.

    Madinat Al Irfane, B.P. 6202, Rabat-Instituts, 10101 Rabat, Morocco

    Open in OpenStreetMap

    Rabat, a UNESCO World Heritage city, combines the historic Medina, the Kasbah of the Udayas and the Hassan Tower with a modern capital that hosts national ministries, universities and the Bouregreg valley redevelopment, making it a natural setting for a conference on 3D city information and smart cities.

    Travel

    By air

    Rabat–Salé Airport (RBA) is about 10 km from the venue, with direct flights from Paris, Madrid, Brussels, Istanbul and other European hubs. Casablanca Mohammed V (CMN), the main intercontinental gateway, is about 100 km away and linked to Rabat by train.

    By train

    ONCF trains run from Casablanca airport to Casa Voyageurs, then frequent services (including the Al Boraq high-speed line) reach Rabat Agdal station, the closest to the venue, in about one hour. Rabat Ville station serves the city centre.

    Getting around

    Blue petits taxis are inexpensive and metered within Rabat. Ride-hailing apps are available. The Rabat–Salé tramway (lines 1 and 2) connects the city centre, the Medina and Salé; the venue is a short taxi ride from the Agdal stops.

    Practical

    Currency: Moroccan dirham (MAD). Time zone: UTC+1. Early-October weather: warm and mostly dry, 18–26 °C. Languages: Arabic and French are widely spoken; English is common in academia and hotels. Plugs: type C/E, 220 V.

    Information

    Visa

    Citizens of the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, Japan and many other countries can enter Morocco without a visa for stays up to 90 days with a passport valid for the duration of the stay. Nationals of other countries may be eligible for the Moroccan e-Visa, or must apply at a Moroccan embassy or consulate.

    Please check the requirements for your nationality well in advance on the official portal of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (acces-maroc.ma) or with the nearest Moroccan consulate.

    Invitation letters

    Registered participants who need a visa can request an official invitation letter from the organising committee. Write to with your full name as in your passport, nationality, passport number, affiliation and, if applicable, your ConfTool paper ID.
