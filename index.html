About

22nd and 11th editions

In October 2027 the 3D GeoInfo and Smart Data and Smart Cities conferences meet in Rabat, at IAV Hassan II, where many of Morocco's surveyors and geomatics engineers are trained. It is the first time the series comes to Africa.

3D GeoInfo, 22nd edition

3D GeoInfo is the ISPRS conference on 3D and 4D geodata. Its papers cover how that data is captured, modelled, managed and analysed, for point clouds, city models, BIM and digital twins.

Smart Data and Smart Cities, 11th edition

SDSC was started by the Urban Data Management Society. It is about what cities do with data: analytics, digital twins, open platforms and the people who use them.

Accepted papers of both conferences are published in the ISPRS Annals and the ISPRS Archives.

    The venue

    IAV Hassan II is in Madinat Al Irfane, Rabat's university district, next to Mohammed V University and the ENSIAS engineering school. Rabat Agdal station is about 3 km away.

    Travel

    By air
    Rabat–Salé airport (RBA) is about 10 km from the venue and has direct flights from Paris, Madrid, Brussels and Istanbul. Casablanca Mohammed V (CMN) is the main international airport, about 100 km away, with trains to Rabat.
    By train
    From Casablanca, ONCF trains reach Rabat Agdal, the station nearest the venue, in about an hour.
    In the city
    The blue petits taxis are metered and cheap. Ride-hailing apps work. The tram links the centre, the medina and Salé.
    Practical
    Currency is the Moroccan dirham (MAD) and the time zone is UTC+1. Early October is usually warm and dry, around 18–26 °C. Arabic and French are spoken everywhere, and English at the university and in hotels. Plugs are type C and E, 220 V.

    Visa

    Citizens of the European Union, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Australia, Japan and many other countries can stay in Morocco for up to 90 days without a visa. Others may be able to use the Moroccan e-Visa, or apply at a Moroccan embassy or consulate. Check the rules for your passport on acces-maroc.ma.

    Invitation letters

    If you need a visa, we can send an invitation letter once you are registered. Write to with your name as it appears in your passport, your nationality, passport number, affiliation and ConfTool paper ID if you have one.
